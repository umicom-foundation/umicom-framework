Compute shared candle studies

Use UmiChartCandleStudyCompute from umicom/chart/candle_study.h with Umicom::chart. It accepts a strictly ordered OHLCV sequence, a study kind and a period.

  1. Supply finite candles with non-negative volume and strictly increasing timestamps.
  2. Allocate the large result on the heap and keep it separate from the input.
  3. Choose a period from 2 to 200. The function publishes the entire result only after success; failure leaves the caller's result unchanged.
  4. Draw only adjacent samples whose valid flags are both true. A zero-volume VWMA window is a deliberate gap.
  5. For a band study, draw lower and upper boundaries as well as the centre. Clip only when projecting to the viewport.
  6. Recompute from retained canonical candles after a provider correction or timeframe change. Never mutate order state while computing or displaying a study.

Weighted closes are scaled before accumulation to avoid overflowing price-times-volume products. Band calculations preserve small differences around a nearby price where possible. Bounds still have to fit a finite double; an unrepresentable boundary returns a capacity error.

Read volume at bar close

  1. Select Volume at bar close in the chart study selector.
  2. Use the period control to choose between 2 and 200 price buckets. In this mode it is a bucket count, not a moving window.
  3. Read the horizontal bars on the right of the chart. Framework assigns each retained candle's volume to the bucket containing that candle's closing price. This approximation does not identify the actual prices of individual trades or separate buying from selling.
  4. The control line marks the middle of the largest bucket. The shaded value area grows from that bucket towards the larger adjacent volume until it covers at least 70% of the total. Ties choose the lower price; whole buckets can take coverage above 70%.
  5. Pan or zoom to inspect the chart: the profile still uses the entire retained history. Changing the timeframe changes the candles used by this approximation. The on-chart label reminds you which calculation is shown.

No positive volume means no control or value-area levels. Equal closing prices produce one bucket. If a range is too narrow for the chosen number of distinct floating-point edges, reduce the bucket count. These studies do not send orders. For individual trade data, Framework also provides UmiChartVolumeProfileCompute, which accepts price and volume observations directly.
