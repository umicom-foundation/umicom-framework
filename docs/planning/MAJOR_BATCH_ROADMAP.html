What this plan means

Batch 42 supplies study planning and a curated guide index. The 46 entries below are future work: 43–54 carry forward the earlier roadmap; 55–88 are a proposed extension introduced here. They are not completed capabilities or promised dates.

A source delivery does not automatically close its wider roadmap milestone. Fixes needed for safety or basic operation move into the first dependent batch. Paper/Live broker access stays read-only and physical writes stay disabled until separately qualified.

Keep the boundaries clear

Reusable behaviour belongs in Framework. Data Server owns databases. Master Controller and Slave Controllers have explicit lifecycles. C23 is primary, Assembly serves appropriate low-level needs, and justified C++ adapters retain a stable C interface. Existing scripts and source implementations remain available until a separate removal decision.

Every delivery brings full files, tests, a beginner guide, a complete project, exercises and answers, validation limits and build/Git steps. Guides and code develop together. The local roots remain Umicom-Applications and umicomOS; publication remains on main.

The practical chains are 43 → 44 → 45 → 46 for developer integration, 47 → 48 for recorded research, 49 → 50 → 51 for trusted deployment, and 52 → 53 for qualified OS media. A required prerequisite can move forward; safety does not wait for a number.

AuthorEngine, Helix and compiler/front-end work are shared platform directions. Proposed digital-asset/UmiCOIN and CCP/custody profiles are not claimed as current submodules. Historical office and medical directions remain unsequenced until their product scope is defined.

54 is a release candidate for a declared subset; it does not cancel the specialised backlog. 88 is a gate for the defined portfolio release, not a promise that no future work will be needed.
